    One Linux host from node_exporter: facts and health tiles, then CPU, memory, disk, network, temperature and pressure rows, plus exporter tabs that appear only where their metrics exist (Proxmox, Docker, kubelet, Batocera, ZFS, NFS, battery, services, updates, logs).
